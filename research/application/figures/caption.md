# Suggested figure caption

**Changes in retrospective failure screening after observing within-problem resubmissions.** Points show H1 minus H0 in percentage points; bars are nominal 95% percentile intervals from 1,000 paired student-cluster bootstrap resamples. All five predictors are evaluated on the same 69,825 observed future-target pairs from 1,500 confirmation students. H0 ends after the first submission of an eligible episode; H1 ends after its last submission. Both checkpoints are supplied the same observed next first-encounter target item. Top row: thresholds selected separately for each model/checkpoint to attain at least 80% failure recall on validation. Bottom row: thresholds selected at the validation top-30% risk cutoff, including ties. Thresholds are held fixed on confirmation; the bottom row does not impose equal or exactly 30% confirmation workloads. Blue markers denote logistic models; orange markers denote three-seed mean-probability GRU ensembles. Workload changes must be considered jointly with failure detection. Intervals condition on the fitted models and validation thresholds and are not adjusted for multiple comparisons. These quantities measure retrospective screening and do not establish learning gains, recommendation quality, equivalence/noninferiority, or intervention effects.

The figure reads only `results_logistic.json` and `results_gru.json`. `screening_changes_data.csv` contains the 20 aggregate estimates and intervals used in the drawing. `figure_manifest.json` records input, script, and output SHA-256 values. Reproduce with `python -X utf8 research/application/figures/plot_screening_changes.py`; no private predictions, model fitting, threshold selection, or screening evaluation are run.
