# Manuscript boundary

The current manuscript is a frozen predictive/screening study, not a completed RL recommender paper. Preserve its hashes; new policy results belong in a separately named draft until deliberately integrated with an amendment.

Use the claim level supported by each experiment: observed prediction, retrospective screening, simulated policy value, or human intervention. Sources are not proof of our implementation; plans are not results. Do not promise SOTA or a venue tier.

When editing a standalone .tex document, keep source editable, open it in Codex and use the built-in compiler. Opening a file does not prove compilation. The historical PDF used a recorded fallback renderer; do not describe it as native LaTeX compilation.
